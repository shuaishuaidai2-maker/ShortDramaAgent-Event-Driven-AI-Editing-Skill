# Example editing plan

## Beat

The line “因为……你父亲，是我杀的” reveals a major secret and changes the relationship between the two characters. Treat it as the scene's peak and the episode's cliffhanger.

## Cut and performance

- Open on the accusation or the reveal line if the source footage has a clean take; otherwise preserve the question as the hook.
- Keep a short, intentional pause after “因为……”. Remove only dead air that does not carry performance.
- Cut to the listener's reaction between the setup and the reveal when usable coverage exists.
- Land the reveal, then cut to black to hold the cliffhanger. Use a white flash only for a true closing beat, not this unresolved ending.

## Sound

- Build with “悬疑弦乐渐强” before the reveal, subject to availability in the local sound catalog.
- Mute music briefly before the key line; place a restrained impact at the reveal.
- Keep the dialogue intelligible and avoid stacking effects over the key words.

## QC

- Confirm the reveal is understandable on first listen.
- Check that the reaction shot and sound cue do not reveal the twist early.
- Confirm the final cut ends on the unresolved moment and does not add a closing white flash.
